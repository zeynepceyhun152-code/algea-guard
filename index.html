The problem

Cyanobacterial harmful algal blooms (HABs) shut down drinking-water intakes and swimming beaches with little warning — toxin levels can spike within a day or two once a bloom takes hold. EPA and state buoys already stream water-quality telemetry in real time, but nobody is turning that stream into a forecast. AlgaeGuard closes that gap: it converts live sensor readings into a next-day bloom-risk score, so lake managers can act (issue an advisory, boost aeration, alert the water plant) before the peak — not after the beach is already closed.
